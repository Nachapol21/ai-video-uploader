Privacy Policy — AI Video Uploader

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Who uses this app

AI Video Uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel it is connected to. It is not offered to other users.

What data the app accesses

  • YouTube upload access (youtube.upload) — to upload videos that the owner has produced to the owner's own channel.
  • Read-only YouTube access (youtube.readonly) — to list the owner's own uploaded videos and their public statistics such as view counts.
  • Read-only YouTube Analytics access (yt-analytics.readonly) — to read performance reports, such as watch time and retention, for the owner's own videos.

How the data is used and stored

Data is used only to upload the owner's videos and to show the owner how those videos perform. Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer or the owner's own cloud server. The app does not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties, and does not use it for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's local token file also removes the app's access.

Google and YouTube policies

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
